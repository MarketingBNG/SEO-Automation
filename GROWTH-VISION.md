# Growth vision (owner: USAIndiaCFO)

Saved on the owner's request, for future sessions to remember. This is the long-term
plan, not a task to build now unless the owner asks.

## The three-AI structure

1. **SEO dashboard** (this app) has its own Assistant.
2. **Social media dashboard** (separate app) has its own Assistant.
3. **Growth Center** (a separate centre the owner has built) holds a **head AI** that
   sits above both. The two dashboard assistants talk to each other and send all
   their reports and analytics up to the Growth Center's AI.

The Growth Center AI:
- Presents all analytics in one place, clearly, like a good presenter, so a new team
  member can understand what is happening, what should happen, and what to change.
- Works on long-term vision and growth: generating leads to drive sales, and making
  the brand more well known.
- Is the single head over the two dashboard assistants. So three main AIs talk to
  each other: SEO assistant, social media assistant, and the Growth Center head AI.

## Order of work (owner's words)

- **Now:** connect the two dashboards (SEO + social media) so their assistants can
  talk and exchange reports. Do NOT build the Growth Center yet.
- **Later:** build the Growth Center head AI that both report up to.
