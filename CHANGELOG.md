# Changelog

What changed in the dashboard, newest first. Claude Code adds a line with every change pushed to
`main`. Format: `- (part) what changed and what it means for the work`, where the part is the one it
affects most: writer, fact-check, ops, seo, publisher, social.

## 2026-11-20
- (ops) Training for the whole team (everyone except the admin), every day: Technical knowledge, Know UIC (our services, how we work, the rules for each, built only from our own pages) and Marketing skills, at associate or manager level. Lessons and tests come with official sources, adaptive levels (up at 80% average, down under 50%), a weekly surprise test, game rounds, slide walkthroughs and checked YouTube videos (Videos library), and a team report with marks, growth and weak topics (CSV). Until today's lessons are read and tests submitted only Associate Training opens; nobody is locked when the day's training was not made, was made after 13:00 IST, or the person is blocked or paused.
- (publisher) Publish checklist: Author, Title, Blog text and Banner must be ticked before approving or publishing (Drafts & Review, Post my blog, calendar, assistant). The banner that was ticked is the one published; a change after ticking undoes that tick.
- (publisher) The assistant can change a live blog's author (from the blog authors list) and edit its words, title or meta through the website, after approval; each change can be undone. Drafts & Review gets "Change author on the live post".
- (ops) Open in Drafts & Review and Check a blog scrolls to the editor; the left sidebar keeps its place when you switch pages.
- (seo) Directory listings and other manual tasks stay on the dashboard after they are ticked: a Done list shows who marked each one and when, the listing's address and notes (never a password), with Mark not done for a wrong tick.

## 2026-11-19
- (ops) Faster under load (local stress test, 1,500 drafts, 800 calendar blogs, 100 people at once): Drafts & Review list, Blog Calendar, Home and the status light are 2 to 4 times faster (India-time dates were formatted the slow way hundreds of times per call). Home sends only what its buttons need. New database indexes keep the progress bar, Learning page and Drafts list fast as the activity log grows. At most two blog covers are drawn at a time, so a page full of covers no longer slows the rest of the dashboard; the same cover asked for many times at once is drawn once. AI credits no longer looks up the dollar-rupee rate on every open page (one lookup at a time, and a 15-minute wait after a failed one).
- (publisher) Two people (or a double press) acting on the same blog at the same moment: Approve is saved and logged once; Approve and Reject on a calendar blog no longer both land (the second gets "Someone else changed this blog at the same moment"); Pause, Resume, Reschedule and Already done get the same protection. A Facts Register mark pressed twice is saved once. Two "New photo cover" presses that both fail no longer lose the existing cover. Adding the same person to the Team twice at once gives a clear message instead of a server error.
- (security) The Learning page (and /api/learning) needs content-work access, and lists only your own assistant questions (the admin sees everyone's). Publishing a Drafts & Review blog needs the website permission, as the assistant's draft_publish does. A blocked person can no longer run paid lookups (SE Ranking research and AI visibility, a fresh "why it changed" explanation). Only an admin can give or take away the admin role. A Facts Register mark takes only verified or needs_verify and always records the signed-in person as reviewer. A bad chat id to the assistant gets a clear error instead of a server error.
- (scheduler) The built-in scheduler never starts a run while the previous one is still going (the Monday steps and banners could run, and pay, twice). It now also frees runs left stuck by a restart every 15 minutes and checks the writing skill's 15-day refresh daily after 03:00 UTC, so the Coolify tasks for /api/cron/sweep and /api/skill/auto-check are optional.
- (ops) Dashboard: Back returns to the previous page of the dashboard. Crawl, audit, keyword and writing-skill dates are shown in India time (IST).
- (publisher) Blog covers made without a photo: a long title is made smaller so it never runs into the "By author" line.

- (publisher) Settings > Website connection: "Remove heading numbers from live posts" (admin) lists the posts already on the website with numbered headings and removes those numbers; only the headings change.

## 2026-11-13
- (publisher) WordPress is removed: blogs are published, updated and given images only through the custom website connector (SITE_CONNECTOR_URL, SITE_CONNECTOR_SECRET). When the connection is not set up nothing is sent, calendar blogs wait with that reason and Settings shows "Website connection". A blog already live is never copied: if its live post cannot be found or read, nothing is sent. WordPress-only work (plugins, Yoast, menus, cache and WebP speed fixes, automatic redirects) is gone; redirects and page fixes become developer tasks. The public site address is SITE_URL (WORDPRESS_SITE_URL still read for now).
- (ops) Assistant: asked for a document, it makes a Word file and gives the download link in the chat (private to the person who asked). It no longer edits the website or saves anything there. After publishing, related-post links go only to posts whose titles really match.
- (seo) Check a blog: a box "What do you want for this blog?" and a "Keep my words and facts" option when uploading. The audit and every rewrite of that blog follow it (for example only SEO and backlinking, content and facts unchanged); wrong facts are still flagged and the request is shown on the audit.
- (ops) Assistant: when Anthropic answers with a temporary server error (for example "503 credential validation failed") the step is tried once more after a few seconds, and if it still fails the message says it is a temporary problem on Anthropic's side, not the key.

Once a day the team's edits become lessons in the writer's memory; the weekly lessons read them too.
- (writer) Uploaded blogs: "Optimise for SEO, AEO and GEO" on a draft, and "Optimise, keep my text" in Check a blog, keep the author's sentences and facts and add only what search and AI answers need (keyword, question headings with direct answers, FAQ, service links, call to action, meta description); the version before it can be restored. A Word file uploaded into a draft shows what it changes compared with the saved blog before anything is saved.

- (ops) Phones: the Blog Calendar shows one card per blog (title, cover, publish time, fact check, status, approve, pause, reschedule) instead of a table that needed sideways scrolling; card headers put the action button under the text instead of squeezing the text into a narrow column (Topics, Associate Training, every page with a header button); taps act at once on touch screens; people with animations turned off in their system get a still dashboard; the viewport covers phone notches. Checked with headless Chrome at 390px.
- (seo) One goal for the whole dashboard: grow client conversions through blogs, SEO, AEO and GEO, built on three pillars, Visibility, Traction and Conversion (lib/goal.ts). The strategy plans each keyword and blog under a pillar (about 40/30/30) and states each pillar's aim for the month; the writer, LinkedIn articles, Topics, Associate Training and the Assistant all start from the goal. Home shows the goal; Monthly Strategy, Blog Calendar and Topics show the pillar.

## 2026-10-28
- (ops) Security audit: Settings can no longer change role access, Testing/Live mode or credit switches (a tester could make itself admin that way); cron routes (daily, weekly, sweep, skill and strategy auto-checks) need the cron secret; meeting transcripts, client insights, account connections, paid live lookups and connection tests are limited to the right roles; private assistant files stay private; 'Check a blog' and site checks no longer fetch private network addresses; audit rewrite and lesson HTML are sanitised.
- (ops) Testing mode and Pause now hold everywhere: calendar blogs, fact checks, topics, training, outreach, covers, reports and the skill refresh wait instead of failing, and pick up from their saved steps when work can run again; tests always run in Live mode and never hang on the queue.
- (ops) No credit spent twice: calendar blog writing and fact checks reuse saved steps after a pause or restart; a failed rewrite of a rejected blog is paused once instead of being paid for again every 15 minutes; Surfer reuses its Content Editor; batch results are not re-sent or re-counted; the autopilot, cron and Publish now can no longer run the same blog at the same time.
- (publisher) Refresh blogs published from Drafts & Review update the page they refresh (address, author and cover kept) instead of creating a new post; 'Save as a separate draft' in Check a blog no longer takes the live post offline; one blog cannot be published twice by two paths at once; publishing without a website connection stops before saving anything and is logged.
- (publisher) Drafts & Review publish also runs IndexNow, sitemap resubmit, Bing, rank tracking and internal links like the calendar does; an analyst cannot publish a calendar blog before its slot; the paid originality check runs only after the free checks pass.
- (writer) The publish gate and the writer's checks now use the same rules (title length, internal links, question answers), so a draft that passes writing is not held at publishing for a rule it was never told.
- (fact-check) An edit by a person, the Assistant or the issue fixer no longer turns a failed fact check into a pass; 'Check and fix' cannot overwrite a save made while it ran.
- (seo) Approving a new strategy stops the old calendar from drafting and publishing, and its remaining blogs stay visible; a strategy cannot be approved twice; rank-drop alerts compare the same search engine; Screaming Frog counts only HTML pages; blog analytics no longer repeats posts or shows a false -100% position change.
- (ops) Data kept tidy: old paid-API cache rows, saved AI steps, progress rows and Surfer editor ids are cleaned up on a schedule; activity log areas, stop actions, sign-in refusals, deletes and publish failures are now logged with the right person.
- (ops) Deletes do what they say: deleting a draft no longer requeues strategy, LinkedIn or live blogs, frees its topic, and stops endless 'Blog held' alerts.
- (ops) Pages: Website Content 'New page' can no longer replace the home page; the Blog rules card loads saved values before saving; draft saves warn instead of overwriting newer edits; pages show an error instead of loading forever; each page fails on its own instead of blanking the dashboard; buttons are shown only to roles that can use them.
- (social) Outreach opening lines are the line itself (no preamble), and one refused lead no longer stops outreach for good.
- (ops) Associate Training: the 09:00 lesson is made later the same day when AI work was paused; a test cannot be scored twice.
